# Expert PvP Battle Evidence Contract

Issue #143 extends the existing owned-only PvP Battle Lab with a conservative expert evidence layer. This layer does not manufacture battle results from IV rank, stat product, CP, or Attack IV.

## Freshness boundary

The browser reads only external snapshots listed in `data/external/index.json` whose category is PvP-related and whose freshness state is `fresh`. The referenced snapshot must also report itself as fresh. Stale or missing data produces no matchup claims.

## Supported normalized matchup row

A row is usable only when it includes enough information to join a result to an owned species/form and an opponent:

- `pokemon_number`, `attacker_pokemon_number`, `dex`, or `attacker_dex`
- `opponent_id`, `opponent`, `defender`, `target`, or `opponent_name`
- explicit `result`/`outcome` of `win`, `loss`, or `tie`, or a numeric battle rating where greater than 500 is a win, less than 500 is a loss, and exactly 500 is a tie

Optional supported fields include attacker form, battle rating, shields, starting energy, starting HP percentage, model version, role, attack breakpoints, and defense bulkpoints.

Unsupported objects remain ignored rather than inferred.

## Exact owned-record mapping

External rows are species/form evidence, not owned-record identities. The browser joins them to each exact canonical owned record with the same Pokédex number and compatible form. This preserves the exact record ID in the displayed comparison while keeping the source evidence separate from collection facts.

Two identical-looking owned records therefore remain distinct rows even when they share the same source matchup evidence.

## Pinned assumptions

A matchup row is excluded when it explicitly conflicts with a requested supported assumption. The initial UI pins:

- 1 shield
- 0 starting energy
- 100% starting HP

An absent assumption in the source is treated as unspecified, not as contradictory. A future simulator can expand this contract without changing existing semantics.

## Derived expert views

From supported rows the client may deterministically calculate:

- wins, losses, and ties per exact owned record
- win rate over the available normalized opponent rows
- opponents that defeat compared records
- whether at least one compared owned record has a source-backed win against a threat
- source-supplied attack breakpoints and defense bulkpoints

These are summaries of supplied evidence. They are not a replacement for a complete turn-by-turn Pokémon GO PvP simulation engine.

## Safety rules

- Rank 1 is never treated as universally best.
- Attack IV alone is never treated as CMP Attack.
- Missing matchup evidence produces an explicit blocked/unknown state.
- Breakpoints and bulkpoints appear only when supplied by the normalized source row.
- Current-meta claims disappear when their snapshot expires.
- No expensive investment recommendation is generated by this layer.

## Remaining #143 scope

This increment establishes the normalized expert evidence and threat-analysis contract. Completing #143 still requires a reviewed, license-safe normalized battle mechanics/model input capable of deterministic turn-by-turn simulation, configurable baiting/stat stages, and complete current-meta matrix generation rather than source-row replay alone.
