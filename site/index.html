Optional field terms can be combined with ordinary search:

name:pikachu form:alolan move:"shadow ball" cp:1500-2500 iv:96-100 level:40+ status:shadow pvp:great -rank:unranked

Unknown or malformed field syntax is treated as ordinary text instead of breaking the search.
