Search syntax

Plain words search across Pokémon name, form, moves, status, dates, and PvP fields.

Use field queries such as name:pikachu, cp:>=1000, iv:>=96, status:shadow, move:"shadow ball", or great:>=98.

Negate a term with -, combine alternatives with OR, and group with parentheses. Example: (status:shadow OR status:purified) -name:rattata.

You can also type requests such as shadow hundos, great league rank 100 or better, or caught in last 30 days.
