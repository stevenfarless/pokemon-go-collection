Data notes

The dashboard is rebuilt from the newest valid filename matching shared-text-YYYY-MM-DD HH_MM_SS.mmm.csv. Older exports remain archived and are excluded from the live page.

PvP percentages are Poke Genie IV rankings for eligible league evolutions. They do not measure the current battle meta. Numeric filters exclude records whose relevant value is missing.
